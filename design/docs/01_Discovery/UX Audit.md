# 06. UX Audit

## Overview

The UX audit evaluates the current PocketPills website to understand how effectively it supports users throughout their healthcare journey.

The audit focuses on the complete experience—from the first interaction on the landing page to task completion and long-term engagement. Rather than evaluating visual design alone, the assessment examines usability, information architecture, navigation, trust, accessibility, and retention.

The goal is to identify friction points, uncover missed opportunities, and establish a foundation for the redesign strategy.

---

# Evaluation Framework

The website was evaluated across the following categories:

- First Impression
- Value Proposition
- Information Architecture
- Navigation
- User Journey
- Conversion

- Content Strategy

# Homepage Analysis

## What Works

- Professional visual design
- Clean layout
- Strong credibility
- Trusted healthcare appearance
- Modern interface
- Clear primary CTA
- Strong review and trust signals

---

## Opportunities

The homepage explains what PocketPills offers.

It does not immediately answer:

> **"How can you help me today?"**

The experience is organized around services rather than user intentions.

Users arrive with specific goals such as:

- I need a doctor.
- I need medication.
- I need a refill.
- I need treatment.
- I need help.

These journeys should become the primary navigation experience.

---

# Value Proposition

## Current

> Complete Healthcare. Delivered.

While accurate, the message is broad and requires users to explore further before understanding whether the platform meets their specific needs.

---

## Opportunity

Communicate outcomes instead of capabilities.

Examples:

- Get treatment without leaving home.
- Refill prescriptions in minutes.
- Manage your family's healthcare in one place.

---

# Information Architecture

## Current State

Navigation is organized around internal services.

Example:

- Treatments
- Online Pharmacy
- About
- Contact

---

## UX Gap

Users naturally think in goals rather than services.

Examples:

- I need help today.
- I need medication.
- I have symptoms.
- I need a prescription.

The information architecture should reflect these mental models.

---

# Navigation

Current navigation is clean but passive.

Users must decide where to begin.

A better experience would immediately guide users toward the correct journey through intent-based entry points.

# Summary

PocketPills has successfully built a reliable digital healthcare platform with strong operational capabilities and high user trust.

However, the current experience is primarily **service-oriented**, requiring users to interpret the platform and determine their own path.

The greatest opportunity is not to add more features, but to redesign the experience around **user intent, guidance, and continuity**.

By shifting from a transactional pharmacy model to a relationship-driven healthcare platform, PocketPills can deliver a more intuitive, personalized, and enduring healthcare experience.
