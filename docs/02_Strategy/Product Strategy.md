# 09. Product Strategy

## Overview

The objective of this redesign is not to create a better online pharmacy.

The objective is to create **Canada's most intuitive digital healthcare platform**—one that guides patients through every stage of their healthcare journey, from the moment they experience a health concern to long-term wellness management.

The product should reduce complexity, eliminate uncertainty, and become the primary destination for managing personal healthcare.

---

# Product Vision

> **Healthcare should feel as simple as opening your favorite app.**

Users should never need to wonder:

- Where do I begin?
- Which service do I need?
- What happens next?
- Who do I contact?

The platform should answer these questions automatically through thoughtful experience design.

---

# Product Mission

To simplify healthcare by creating a connected platform that helps Canadians discover, access, manage, and improve their health through one seamless digital experience.

---

# Product Philosophy

The redesign is built around one central belief:

> **People don't want healthcare services. They want healthcare solutions.**

Users don't care whether they need:

- A consultation
- A pharmacy
- Insurance support
- Medication delivery

They care about solving their health problem.

The platform should organize itself around that goal.

---

# Product Principles

## 1. Intent Before Features

Every interaction begins with understanding why the user is here.

Not what the business offers.

---

## 2. Guide, Don't Navigate

The platform should recommend the next best action instead of asking users to figure it out.

---

## 3. One Journey, One Platform

Every healthcare interaction should feel connected.

Users should never feel like they are moving between separate products.

---

## 4. Personalization by Default

Healthcare experiences should adapt to each individual.

The more users engage, the smarter and more helpful the platform becomes.

---

## 5. Care Doesn't End at Delivery

Medication delivery marks the beginning of ongoing care—not the end.

---

# Strategy Summary

This product strategy is built around four transformational shifts.

| Current Product | Future Product |
|-----------------|----------------|
| Online Pharmacy | Digital Healthcare Platform |
| Feature Navigation | Intent Navigation |
| Transactions | Relationships |
| Reactive Care | Continuous Care |

The product is no longer defined by the services it offers.

It is defined by **how effortlessly it helps people navigate their healthcare journey.**
