# 22. Usability Testing & Validation

## Overview

Usability testing validates whether the redesigned PocketPills experience enables users to complete healthcare tasks efficiently, confidently, and without unnecessary friction.

The objective is to ensure that the redesigned platform not only improves visual design but also delivers measurable improvements in usability, accessibility, task completion, and user confidence.

Testing focuses on validating the complete healthcare journey—from discovering care to long-term health management.

---

# Testing Objectives

The usability study aims to validate whether users can:

- Identify the correct healthcare journey.
- Navigate the platform without assistance.
- Complete healthcare tasks efficiently.
- Understand system feedback.
- Trust the platform throughout the experience.
- Continue using the platform after treatment.

---

# Research Questions

The study investigates the following questions:

1. Can users identify the correct entry point within 30 seconds?
2. Do users understand the healthcare journey?
3. Is navigation intuitive?
4. Can users complete consultations without confusion?
5. Does the dashboard support ongoing healthcare management?
6. Does the redesign increase trust and confidence?
7. Would users return to the platform for future healthcare needs?

---

# Participants

Target participant groups include:

- Busy professionals
- Parents and caregivers
- Chronic care patients
- Seniors
- First-time digital healthcare users

Testing should represent a broad range of healthcare needs and digital literacy levels.

---

# Test Scenarios

## Scenario 01

### Find Treatment

Task

A user experiencing symptoms needs to identify the appropriate treatment.

Success Criteria

The user reaches the recommended treatment without external assistance.

---

## Scenario 02

### Book a Consultation

Task

Book an online consultation and complete the medical questionnaire.

Success Criteria

The consultation is scheduled without confusion or errors.

---

## Scenario 03

### Complete a Prescription Order

Task

Review a prescription, apply insurance, complete payment, and track delivery.

Success Criteria

The user understands every step before confirming the order.

---

## Scenario 04

### Manage Medication

Task

Locate active medications, review dosage instructions, and set reminders.

Success Criteria

The user manages medications confidently within the dashboard.

---

## Scenario 05

### Family Care

Task

Switch to another family member and review their appointments and medications.

Success Criteria

Users easily distinguish between family profiles and complete tasks correctly.

# Success Criteria

The redesign is considered successful if:

- Users complete primary tasks without assistance.
- Navigation requires minimal learning.
- Healthcare information is easily understood.
- Users consistently know the next step.
- Users express confidence in the platform.

---

# Accessibility Validation

The platform should be evaluated for:

- Keyboard navigation
- Screen reader compatibility
- Color contrast
- Touch target size
- Text scalability
- Reduced motion preferences

Accessibility testing should occur alongside usability testing rather than afterward.

---

The redesign is expected to demonstrate improvements in:

### Discoverability

Users identify healthcare pathways more quickly through intent-based navigation.

---

### Decision Making

Contextual guidance reduces uncertainty during consultations and treatment selection.

---

### Task Completion

Simplified workflows reduce unnecessary steps and improve completion rates.

---

### Trust

Transparent pricing, timelines, and healthcare guidance increase confidence throughout the journey.

---

### Retention

The personalized dashboard encourages continued engagement beyond prescription fulfillment.
