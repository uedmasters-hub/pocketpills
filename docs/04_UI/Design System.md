# 16. Design System

## Overview

The PocketPills Design System establishes a scalable visual and interaction language that supports the evolution of PocketPills from a digital pharmacy into Canada's leading digital healthcare platform.

Rather than redefining the brand, the design system evolves the existing PocketPills identity by preserving its strongest attributes—trust, simplicity, accessibility, and warmth—while introducing a more product-focused experience.

The system provides a shared foundation for product design, engineering, and future healthcare services.

---

# Design Philosophy

Healthcare should feel:

- Simple
- Calm
- Trustworthy
- Personal
- Intelligent

The interface should reduce anxiety, guide decisions, and make healthcare management feel effortless.

Rather than feeling like a pharmacy website, the experience should feel like a trusted healthcare companion.

## Neutral Palette

Large white surfaces create breathing room.

Neutral grays establish hierarchy without visual noise.

Use:

- White
- Soft Gray
- Surface Gray
- Border Gray
- Text Gray
- Dark Gray

Avoid pure black whenever possible.

Illustrations should depict:

- Real healthcare situations
- Families
- Recovery
- Preventive care
- Diverse communities

- Overly technical diagrams
- Generic stock illustrations

---

# Photography

Photography should feel authentic.

Show:

- Real people
- Everyday healthcare
- Diverse Canadians
- Families
- Clinicians
- Home environments

Avoid staged corporate photography.

---

# Motion Principles

Animation should communicate meaning.

Use motion to:

- Confirm actions
- Explain transitions
- Indicate progress
- Reinforce hierarchy

Avoid decorative animation.

# Design Language

The redesign evolves PocketPills from a marketing website into a product experience.

The visual language should emphasize:

- Large white surfaces
- Purple-led branding
- Calm healthcare aesthetics
- Healthcare-specific components
- Editorial typography
- Soft illustrations
- Rounded interfaces
- Product cards
- Personalized dashboards

The experience should feel modern, trustworthy, and distinctly PocketPills—not like a generic SaaS application or a traditional pharmacy website.

---

# Design System Success

The design system succeeds when:

- Every screen feels like PocketPills.
- Every interaction reinforces trust.
- Healthcare feels approachable.
- Components scale across future products.
- Users spend less time learning the interface and more time managing their health.

Ultimately, the design system should enable a healthcare experience that is consistent, accessible, scalable, and unmistakably recognizable as PocketPills.
