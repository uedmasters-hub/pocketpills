# 💊 PocketPills UX Redesign

> **Transforming PocketPills from Canada's leading online pharmacy into a comprehensive digital healthcare platform.**

---

# Overview

This repository documents the complete end-to-end redesign of the PocketPills digital healthcare experience.

The project follows a structured **Product Design Process**, beginning with business understanding and research, progressing through UX strategy and interface design, and concluding with interactive prototyping, usability validation, and project reflection.

Rather than focusing only on visual improvements, this redesign rethinks how Canadians discover healthcare, consult healthcare professionals, receive medication, and manage their long-term health.

---

# Design Challenge

PocketPills has successfully simplified online pharmacy services for Canadians.

However, as digital healthcare evolves, patients increasingly expect integrated healthcare experiences—not isolated pharmacy transactions.

This redesign explores how PocketPills can evolve into a connected healthcare ecosystem while preserving its strongest brand attributes:

- Trust
- Simplicity
- Accessibility
- Convenience

---

# Project Objectives

The redesign aims to:

- Improve healthcare discoverability
- Reduce cognitive load
- Simplify healthcare journeys
- Increase patient confidence
- Strengthen long-term engagement
- Improve medication adherence
- Create a scalable healthcare platform
- Support future healthcare services

# Design Principles

The redesign follows five guiding principles:

- **Intent Before Features**
- **Guide, Don't Overwhelm**
- **Reduce Cognitive Load**
- **Build Trust Through Transparency**
- **Support Continuous Care**

These principles inform every product and design decision throughout the project.

# Final Vision

The redesign envisions PocketPills as more than an online pharmacy.

It becomes a trusted digital healthcare companion where Canadians can:

- Find the right care
- Consult licensed professionals
- Manage medications
- Track health progress
- Support family healthcare
- Build healthier habits over time

The result is a connected healthcare experience designed around people—not healthcare systems.
