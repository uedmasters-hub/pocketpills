# 21. Prototype & Interaction Design

## Overview

The interactive prototype transforms the final user interface into a realistic, end-to-end healthcare experience.

Rather than presenting isolated screens, the prototype simulates how users discover care, complete healthcare tasks, receive medication, and manage their ongoing health through connected interactions.

The prototype serves three primary purposes:

- Validate user experience before development
- Demonstrate complete healthcare journeys
- Support stakeholder reviews and usability testing

---

# Prototype Goals

The prototype should validate:

- Navigation
- User flows
- Information architecture
- Task completion
- Interaction patterns
- Accessibility
- Overall usability

Every interaction should reinforce the feeling of a connected healthcare experience.

# Prototype Summary

The interactive prototype brings the redesigned PocketPills experience to life by connecting every screen, interaction, and healthcare journey into one cohesive product.

Rather than validating isolated interfaces, the prototype evaluates the complete patient experience—from the first health concern to ongoing care management.

This ensures the platform is intuitive, trustworthy, accessible, and ready for usability testing before engineering implementation.
