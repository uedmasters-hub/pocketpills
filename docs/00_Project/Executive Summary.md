# 01. Executive Summary

## Project Overview

PocketPills is one of Canada's leading digital pharmacy platforms, offering prescription fulfillment, virtual consultations, medication management, and home delivery. Over time, the company has expanded beyond pharmacy services toward becoming a broader digital healthcare provider.

Despite offering a comprehensive suite of healthcare services, the current digital experience primarily communicates individual features rather than guiding users through a connected healthcare journey. New users must interpret the platform's offerings themselves, resulting in unnecessary cognitive load and missed opportunities for engagement.

This project reimagines PocketPills as a modern, patient-centered healthcare platform by shifting the experience from **transactional service delivery** to **continuous healthcare management**.

---

## Background

Healthcare experiences in Canada remain fragmented. Patients often navigate multiple providers, clinics, pharmacies, insurance systems, and communication channels to accomplish a single healthcare task.

PocketPills has already solved many operational challenges by combining telemedicine, pharmacy services, medication delivery, and prescription management into a single platform. However, the digital experience does not yet fully communicate this value or support users throughout their complete healthcare journey.

The redesign aims to bridge this gap through improved information architecture, user-centered navigation, personalized experiences, and a stronger long-term product vision.

---

## Problem Statement

The current experience is feature-oriented rather than journey-oriented.

Users arrive with specific healthcare needs such as:

- Finding a doctor
- Renewing a prescription
- Managing medication
- Seeking treatment
- Understanding insurance

Instead of immediately guiding users toward these goals, the platform presents services as isolated offerings. This creates friction during discovery, weakens the value proposition, and limits long-term engagement.

Additionally, the product experience concludes shortly after medication delivery, leaving significant opportunities for continued patient support, proactive healthcare, and long-term retention.

---

## Project Goals

The redesign focuses on five strategic objectives:

- Simplify healthcare discovery through intent-driven user journeys.
- Position the platform as a complete digital healthcare ecosystem rather than only an online pharmacy.
- Improve trust, clarity, and decision-making throughout the patient journey.
- Increase user retention through continuous healthcare management.
- Establish a scalable design system capable of supporting future healthcare services.

---

## Design Vision

To create Canada's most intuitive digital healthcare platform—one that feels personal, proactive, and always within reach.

The experience should help users spend less time navigating healthcare systems and more time focusing on their wellbeing.

Rather than asking users to understand healthcare services, the platform should understand user intentions and guide them toward the appropriate care.

---

## Expected Outcomes

### Business Outcomes

- Higher conversion rates
- Improved customer retention
- Increased lifetime value
- Stronger brand differentiation
- Expansion beyond pharmacy services

### User Outcomes

- Reduced cognitive load
- Faster task completion
- Higher trust and confidence
- Improved medication adherence
- Better long-term healthcare engagement

---

## Design Principles

The redesign is guided by five principles:

1. Intent before features.
2. Simplicity over complexity.
3. Guidance over navigation.
4. Continuity over transactions.
5. Healthcare that is always within reach.
