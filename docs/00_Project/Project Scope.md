# 02. Project Scope

## Project Overview

This project is a strategic redesign of PocketPills with the goal of transforming it from a digital pharmacy into a modern, patient-centric healthcare platform.

Rather than focusing solely on improving the visual interface, this redesign explores the complete healthcare experience—from the moment a user identifies a health concern to long-term care management and retention.

The project combines product strategy, user experience, information architecture, branding, and interface design to create a scalable foundation for the future of digital healthcare in Canada.

---

## Project Objectives

The redesign aims to:

- Reposition PocketPills as a complete healthcare platform rather than only an online pharmacy.
- Simplify healthcare access through intuitive and guided user journeys.
- Reduce friction across consultation, prescription, pharmacy, and follow-up experiences.
- Increase user trust through transparent, personalized, and supportive interactions.
- Improve long-term engagement by extending the experience beyond medication delivery.
- Build a scalable design system capable of supporting future healthcare services.

## Out of Scope

The following areas are intentionally excluded from this redesign:

- Clinical workflows
- Pharmacy inventory management
- Internal administrative systems
- Backend architecture
- Medical diagnosis logic
- Government healthcare policy
- Third-party integrations implementation
- Development and engineering execution

---

## Assumptions

This project assumes:

- Users expect healthcare experiences comparable to modern consumer apps.
- Digital healthcare adoption in Canada will continue to increase.
- Personalized experiences improve engagement and retention.
- Users value simplicity more than feature quantity.
- AI will increasingly assist healthcare navigation without replacing clinicians.

---

## Design Challenges

Several challenges were identified before beginning the redesign:

- Healthcare services are fragmented across multiple systems.
- The current platform emphasizes features over user goals.
- New users struggle to identify the appropriate starting point.
- Trust must be established quickly in a healthcare context.
- Different user groups require different care journeys.
- The product lacks a compelling post-treatment experience.

---

## Success Criteria

The redesign will be considered successful if it:

### Business

- Improves conversion from visitor to patient.
- Increases user retention.
- Strengthens brand differentiation.
- Supports future service expansion.

### User Experience

- Reduces cognitive load.
- Enables faster task completion.
- Improves trust throughout the journey.
- Provides clear guidance at every stage.
- Encourages long-term healthcare engagement.

---

## Guiding Principles

Every design decision throughout this project is evaluated against five principles:

### 1. Intent Before Features

Design around what users are trying to accomplish—not around internal product categories.

---

### 2. Simplicity Before Complexity

Healthcare should feel approachable, understandable, and effortless.

---

### 3. Guidance Before Choice

Reduce decision fatigue by helping users find the right path instead of presenting every option equally.

---

### 4. Care Beyond Transactions

Medication delivery is one milestone in a much larger healthcare journey.

---

### 5. Design for Trust

Every interaction should reinforce confidence, clarity, and reassurance through transparency, consistency, and accessibility.
